Thicket: Match Adventure: Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Thicket: Match Adventure ("Thicket", "the game") is a match-3 game made by Scott Glasser ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: Thicket has no accounts, no ads and no tracking across other apps, and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives and settings) is saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this saved progress.

Anonymous gameplay analytics

To find out which levels are too hard or too easy and to fix problems, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to our analytics provider, PostHog. These include:

  • when the game is opened, and the furthest level reached;
  • when a level is started and how it ended (won, lost or quit), with the moves and score;
  • when a booster is used;
  • when an in-app purchase is made (which item and its price, not your payment details);
  • if the game unexpectedly restarts, what it was doing just before.

These events are linked to a random identifier created on your device, not to you. We also record the app version and the platform (iOS). As with any internet connection, PostHog receives your device's IP address when events are sent, which it may use to estimate an approximate country or region. We don't use analytics to identify you, and we don't sell this information or use it for advertising.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. To confirm purchases and deliver what you bought, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase records (which items were bought and when) from Apple.

Service providers

  • PostHog: anonymous gameplay analytics (servers in the United States).
  • RevenueCat: purchase validation.
  • Apple: the App Store, payments and TestFlight.

Tracking

Thicket does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not show ads, and does not share information with data brokers.

Children

Thicket is a family-friendly game. It does not ask for or knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

Anonymous analytics are kept only as long as they're useful for improving the game. Because they aren't linked to your identity, we can't look up a specific player's data, but if you'd like us to try, or have any other request, email us and we'll help.

Beta testing

If you test Thicket through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share your name, email, device details, crash reports and any feedback you send with us, as described in Apple's privacy policy. We use these only to improve the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
